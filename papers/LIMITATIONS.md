# LIMITATIONS & SCOPE STATEMENT

## 1. Domain Scope
Current HSCI benchmarks evaluate mathematical, formal logic, and HTN planning tasks. Evaluation on open-world robotics or unconstrained conversational dialogue remains future work.

## 2. Benchmark Sample Size
Benchmark manifests currently execute $N=30$ runs per suite ($N=240$ total executions). Scaling to $N \ge 100$ is planned for extended longitudinal studies.

## 3. Real-World Multi-Agent Deployment
HSCI operates as a single-agent neurosymbolic loop (`RIRLoop`). Multi-agent collaborative consensus is out of scope for the current architecture.
