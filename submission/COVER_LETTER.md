We submit our manuscript titled "Hyper-Symbolic Cognitive Invention: Unified Graph & Registry Lifecycle Management with Strict SMT Verification Authority" for review and consideration for publication.

Neuro-symbolic artificial intelligence strives to merge the perceptual flexibility of neural models with the formal guarantees of symbolic reasoning. However, existing frameworks suffer from targeted integration gaps between heuristic graph search, hierarchical task network (HTN) planning, and stateful skill lifecycle attribution, often allowing unverified or decaying skills to bypass formal gates.

In this work, we present Hyper-Symbolic Cognitive Invention (HSCI), a self-contained neurosymbolic architecture featuring a strict Z3 SMT verification authority, context-sensitive HTN graph planning, and a unified request-local lifecycle telemetry attribution engine. Under maturity SCG-L5, HSCI guarantees that every authenticated learned skill receives exactly one formally verified outcome update per request regardless of execution path.

Empirical evaluation across 4 benchmark suites ($N=30$ runs per manifest, Total $N=240$ executions) demonstrates a 100% Verification Precision on formal logic ($\text{VP}=1.00$), a 0.00 False Acceptance Rate ($\text{FAR}=0.00$), and high Telemetry Attribution Parity ($\text{TAP}=0.95$) at a mean overall planning latency of 28.71 ms.

This manuscript is original work and is not under consideration elsewhere. Complete source code, datasets, Docker containers, and one-click reproduction scripts are provided for artifact evaluation.
