# Shared seed knowledge for the __common__ world.
#
# Every line is ordinary English and is learned through exactly the same parser,
# consistency check and knowledge base as anything a user types. Nothing here is
# special-cased in code: edit, extend or delete freely. Users can override or add
# to it in their own worlds.

# --- Lexical knowledge: comparatives that are opposites (enables "who is the youngest?")
Younger is the opposite of older.
Shorter is the opposite of taller.
Smaller is the opposite of bigger.
Smaller is the opposite of larger.
Lighter is the opposite of heavier.
Slower is the opposite of faster.
Weaker is the opposite of stronger.
Colder is the opposite of hotter.
Lower is the opposite of higher.
Poorer is the opposite of richer.
Earlier is the opposite of later.
Cheaper is the opposite of more expensive.

# --- Physics formulas (quantities chain: e.g. mass + velocity -> momentum)
Force equals mass times acceleration.
Momentum equals mass times velocity.
Kinetic energy equals half of mass times velocity squared.
Potential energy equals mass times gravitational acceleration times height.
Weight equals mass times gravitational acceleration.
Speed equals distance divided by time.
Density equals mass divided by volume.
Pressure equals force divided by area.
Work equals force times displacement.
Power equals work divided by duration.
Voltage equals current times resistance.
Electrical power equals voltage times current.
